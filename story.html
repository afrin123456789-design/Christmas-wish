A letter is a small journey.

Here's how one little Christmas letter is written, sealed into a link, and opened by someone it was meant for.

Merry Christmas

Chapter 1 of 5

  1. Chapter 01

    A cold night, a warm thought.

    Somewhere someone is thinking of you. The first step is noticing it, and letting it become a few words on paper.

  2. Chapter 02

    Write it the way you'd say it.

    A letter doesn't need to be perfect. It needs to be true. Keep it short, keep it kind, and let your own voice do the work.

  3. Chapter 03

    Seal it into a link.

    The whole letter is packed into a single link. There's no account and no upload: the words travel inside the link itself.

  4. Chapter 04

    Opened on the other side.

    They tap the link. The box opens, the envelope unfolds, and the letter appears exactly as you wrote it.

  5. Chapter 05

    Then they pass it on.

    The best letters get shared. A letter you received can become the one you send next, and the warmth keeps travelling.
